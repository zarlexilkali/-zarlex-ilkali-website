Meet the Artist

A sound of his own.

Born Precious Samuel, Zarlex Ilkali is a Nigerian Afro-fusion artist based in Lagos. His sound blends contemporary Afrobeats, modern bounce, and street-inspired storytelling.

His debut single, BIG MOTION, introduces an artist driven by ambition, authenticity, and the hustle to rise beyond his beginnings. Inspired by Lagos and a new generation of African music, Zarlex is building his own sound and working towards a global audience.

The journey is just beginning. The motion never stops. 🗿
